# Surface brief: app/index.html (the whole SafeDive Coach app, nine screens)

Scope: the complete phone web app, all nine screens plus the consent sheet. Mode: Operate (a parent completes one job: film, wait, read one tip). Audience, job and constraints live in PRODUCT.md; this brief holds only the direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SafeDive Coach is printed, not rendered: a two-ink risograph flyer from the town pool's recreation department, and the overlap of the two inks is the measurement. It refuses the category default of white cards, one blue, soft shadows and a hero-metric block.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground (#f6f1e4), aqua ink (#00a6c8) and sun-orange ink (#ff6a2a); words in ink black (#1a1a1a); the overprint of aqua over orange (#004521) is the only "good" colour. No greys for secondary text: tints of ink on paper. Display face Bricolage Grotesque (variable, wide, 800) for headings and every number; Atkinson Hyperlegible for body, chosen for sunlight legibility. Rules are 2 to 3 px ink, square corners (radius 0 to 4 px), flat fills, no shadows. Standard web controls: inputs with 2 px ink borders, a solid-orange primary button, outlined secondary, bottom tab bar ruled in ink with an orange active bar. Light only: a sunlit deck has no dark mode.

STORY: The parent sees a bold printed program, films one lap, watches the inks read the swimmer, and leaves with one number, one tip and one drill, with the evidence one scroll below.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Home, 390 wide): masthead "SafeDive Coach" at 30 px with the level as an aqua stamp ring at right; a 236 px aqua-duotone photo of the real backstroke lap with the headline "Film one lap. Fix one thing." in paper on an orange overprint band; a ruled three-cell strip (laps, streak, best) with 34 px orange numbers; name and stroke fields; the 58 px orange "Record or choose a video" button; outlined "See what the app sees"; fine print; four-tab bar.

FORM: Parks & Rec pool flyer, candidate 4 of the grounded list (behind the swim-level card, the broadcast lane overlay and painted deck signage). seed:24be08711fa4351e

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Overprint as measurement. Every meter draws the target zone in aqua and the swimmer's reading in orange with multiply blending; where they overlap the ink turns deep green. The video overlay draws the torso line in orange and the target line in aqua; the angle chart fills the target band in aqua and draws the scored stretch in full orange, unscored frames as ink hairlines. Motion grammar: one authored moment, the orange reading bar growing into the aqua band on the results screen (exponential ease-out, 600 ms); screen changes are a 160 ms fade-rise; nothing else moves.

RAISES: from Emission-Line Rail, every reading registers against one rail, the flat line; from Midnight Transit, one series burns brightest; from Star Atlas, a fixed five-step size ramp carries hierarchy; from Centre-Rail Setting, three values only, state is overprint never a fourth colour.

HONEST RISK: Fluorescent orange and aqua can read as summer-camp cute; the cure is scale and restraint, inks used only where they measure.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
